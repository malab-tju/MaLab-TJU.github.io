RESEARCH GROUP · TIANJIN UNIVERSITY

Embodied Intelligence & Autonomous Systems

Xiaoguang Ma is a Beiyang Chair Professor at Tianjin University's School of Artificial Intelligence and supervises Ph.D. and master's students. His research spans Physical AI, Agentic AI, AI4Engr, and unmanned systems.

Our group studies embodied robotics, aerial vision-language navigation, and agent-driven robotic systems, with a focus on perception, learning, and autonomous decision-making in physical environments.

We welcome inquiries from postdoctoral researchers, Ph.D. and master's students, and undergraduate research interns interested in embodied intelligence and AI agents.

Research

01

Embodied Robotics

We study imitation learning and the links between perception and action to develop intelligent robotic behavior for real-world tasks.

02

Aerial Embodied Intelligence

We combine visual and spatial reasoning for aerial vision-language navigation, studying how unmanned systems understand their environments and navigation instructions.

03

Agentic AI for Robotics

We explore how multimodal large models can support robotic systems, developing navigation and decision-making frameworks that learn autonomously and improve over time.
