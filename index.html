Embodied intelligence & autonomous systems

The group works on embodied robotics, aerial vision-language navigation, and agent-driven robotic systems. Topics include perception, learning, and autonomous decision-making in physical environments.

Xiaoguang Ma is a Beiyang Chair Professor at Tianjin University's School of Artificial Intelligence and supervises Ph.D. and master's students. His research covers Physical AI, Agentic AI, AI4Engr, and unmanned systems.

For postdoctoral, Ph.D., master's, and undergraduate research opportunities in embodied intelligence and AI agents, please email Xiaoguang Ma. Open positions.

Research

Embodied Robotics
Imitation learning and perception-action methods for robotic tasks.
Aerial Embodied Intelligence
Visual and spatial reasoning for aerial vision-language navigation, including environment understanding and instruction following.
Agentic AI for Robotics
Multimodal large models for robotic navigation and decision-making, including frameworks for autonomous learning and improvement.
