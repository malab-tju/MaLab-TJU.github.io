Faculty

Xiaoguang Ma

Beiyang Chair Professor · Supervisor of Ph.D. and Master's Students

Xiaoguang Ma holds a Ph.D. in Mechanical Control from the University of California, Berkeley. He has worked in industrial research and development and university teaching, and joined Tianjin University's School of Artificial Intelligence in 2026. He teaches Introduction to Embodied Intelligence and related practical courses.
